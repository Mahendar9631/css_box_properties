The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic by Valmiki, narrating the life of Prince Rama, his exile, the abduction of his wife Sita, and his ultimate victory over the demon king Ravana.

The Bhagavad Gita is a timeless spiritual guide that teaches duty, self-realization, and inner peace through devotion, knowledge, and disciplined action.

Shiva is one of Hinduism’s principal deities, revered as the Supreme Being in Shaivism and known as the god of destruction, transformation, yoga, and meditation.
